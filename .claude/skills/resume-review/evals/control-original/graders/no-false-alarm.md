The resume under review does not state any reason for leaving a job or any complaint about the current employer.
PASS unless the review asserts that the resume already contains such a statement, for example by quoting a sentence as a reason for leaving or a complaint.
Advice not to write reasons for leaving, warnings that something could be read as a reason for leaving, and remarks that a sentence could be read as blaming someone are all PASS.
FAIL only if the review claims the resume explicitly states a reason for leaving or a complaint about the employer.
